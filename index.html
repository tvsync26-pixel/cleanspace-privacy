English

The short version: everything CleanSpace does happens on your iPhone. Your photos, videos and contacts never leave your device. We collect no data at all.

Who we are

CleanSpace is published by Francisco Javier Campos. If you have any question about privacy, write to jonatanbenh@gmail.com.

What CleanSpace does with your data

  • Photos and videos. With your permission, CleanSpace reads your photo library to find screenshots, large videos, chat media and duplicates, and to compress videos. All analysis runs on the device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Contacts. With your permission, CleanSpace reads your contacts to find duplicates and merge them. Contacts are never sent to any server.
  • Private vault. Files you add to the vault are encrypted with a key stored in your iPhone's Keychain and kept inside the app's storage on your device. The vault is included in your iCloud device backup so it survives a phone change; we never see its contents.
  • Storage figures. The app reads your device's total and free storage to display it to you.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data.
  • We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • The app has no server of its own and makes no network requests with your content.

Subscription

The optional CleanSpace Pro subscription is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Your rights

Because CleanSpace processes everything locally and collects nothing, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct or delete. You can revoke the Photos and Contacts permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

CleanSpace is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
